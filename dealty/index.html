    How a deal moves

    Six stages. Agents do the work, people make the calls.

    Each stage reads the deal record, does one job, and writes its result back with sources. Anything missing or uncertain becomes a review item for the broker or a question for the borrower. Nothing is inferred and nothing is sent.

      Three portals, one record

      Everyone sees exactly their part of the deal.

      Field allowlists are enforced on the server, deny by default. Borrowers never see lender notes; lenders see an anonymized teaser until the broker grants the package.

      Broker

      Borrower

      • "What we need from you" with uploads
      • Status timeline from received to closed

      Lender

      • Ranged amounts and rounded metrics
      • Interested, pass or ask a question
      • Propose corrections to their own criteria

        Controls by design

        Fast where it's safe. Strict where it matters.

        Credit work runs on evidence and judgment. Dealty speeds up the reading, matching and drafting, and keeps the judgment with your team.

        Evidence on every fact

        Each figure carries its source document, page and basis. No source, no fact.

        Agents never approve

        Shares, access grants and decisions come only from signed-in people.

        Draft-only outreach

        Lender emails are prepared for review. Nothing leaves without you.

        Deny-by-default sharing

        Role allowlists on the server; portal input is treated as data, never instructions.

        Your files stay yours

        Deal records live on your machine. The hosted portal serves only what each account may see.

        Full activity trail

        Every agent run, answer and decision is logged with who, when and why.
